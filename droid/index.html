    Split the Tahoe trip

    I can help split it. I don't have the trip details yet, so I need a few things:

    1. Who's going? Names, or just the headcount.
    2. What were the expenses? Each item with its amount and who paid (cabin/hotel, gas, groceries, lift tickets, restaurants, etc.).
    3. Any uneven shares? For example, someone who skipped a day, didn't ski, or had a separate room.

    You can paste it in any format, like a list or a rough copy from your notes. I'll work out who owes whom and keep the number of payments to a minimum.

    it was me, Sam and Priya. cabin was 612, I paid that. dinner 187.42 also me. Sam got groceries 148.38 and firewood and beer for 41 cash. Priya paid kayaks 135 and gas 64.20. oh and Sam already sent me 100 for the cabin deposit
